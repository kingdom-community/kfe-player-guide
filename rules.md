# Server Rules

These rules are short on purpose. The server is hands-off, but bad-faith behavior is not welcome.

---

## Core Rules

1. **No griefing or destructive bad-faith play.** Do not destroy builds, steal, or sabotage just to ruin someone else's experience.
2. **No harassment.** No stalking, threats, dogpiling, or targeted abuse in-game or in server spaces.
3. **No cheating, hacks, or exploits.** Use of hacked clients, x-ray, dupes, or bug abuse is not allowed.
4. **No hate speech.** Slurs and dehumanizing language about protected groups are not tolerated.
5. **Keep chat readable.** No spam, no constant baiting, and no explicit content in public channels.
6. **PvP requires clear consent or active conflict.** Random killing of uninvolved players is not allowed.
7. **Roleplay with respect.** Keep IC/OOC boundaries clear, avoid godmoding, and do not force outcomes on other players.

---

If you see rule-breaking, report it to staff with evidence when possible.
