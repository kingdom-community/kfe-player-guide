# Getting Started

Kingdom: First Era is a casual, unwhitelisted survival roleplay server. No application. No whitelist. Join and survive.

---

## What This Server Is

You are in the First Era — the oldest period in the history of this world. There are no kingdoms here. No laws. No gods walking beside you. The land is young and violent, and civilization is barely a rumor in the south.

This server is part of the Greater Kingdom Community, a long-running Minecraft roleplay community with established lore and history. The First Era is the earliest, least-documented period in that canon. The mystery is intentional.

---

## What You Can Do

- Form a tribe with other players, or survive alone
- Tame wild animals and build a life outside of conflict
- Roleplay — create a character, engage with the setting, build something
- Fight, explore, build, or disappear into the wilderness

There is no quest. There is no story waiting for you. What you do here is the story.

---

## What Staff Will and Won't Do

**Staff will:** handle players who harass others or act in bad faith. Report problems and they will be addressed.

**Staff won't:** run your story, intervene in player conflicts, or tell you what to do. The server is designed to run itself.

Read the full player rules here: [`rules.md`](rules.md).
